Step 2

Taxable benefit (company car taxation) 🇩🇪 Germany only

This step applies to Germany only — other countries follow different tax rules and are not covered here. In Germany, the private use of a company car is treated as a non-cash benefit ("geldwerter Vorteil") and taxed monthly as a percentage of the car's gross list price ("Bruttolistenpreis").

Notes

Deduction rates (Step 1) — based on private-use category and vehicle type:

  • #1 Mainly business use (0–40% private use): 20% ICE / 15% EV
  • #2 Balanced use (41–60% private use): 40% ICE / 35% EV
  • #3 Mainly private use (>60% private use): 60% ICE / 55% EV
  • #4 Non-Sales (applies regardless of usage split): 75% ICE / 70% EV

Taxation rates (Step 2) — current German rules as of 2026:

  • ICE: 1% of gross list price per month
  • EV with gross list price ≤ €100,000: 0.25% per month
  • EV with gross list price > €100,000: 0.5% per month

Plug-in hybrids follow separate rules (0.5% if minimum electric range/CO₂ criteria are met, otherwise the full 1%) and are not covered by this calculator.

Step 3 is a simplified illustration, not a payroll or tax calculation. It assumes the lease rate is deducted from gross salary (salary conversion) and applies your assumed rate only to the additional taxable benefit. It does not account for individual tax class, allowances, church tax, or social security contribution caps.
