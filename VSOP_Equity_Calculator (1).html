Both rules are minimums. A grant that lands exactly on the percentage qualifies, as does exactly 6.000 € in the voluntary program. Whichever of the two is higher is binding, and there is no upper ceiling.

Grants are set in 500 € steps, always rounded up.

The percentage is measured against the OTE equivalent, base plus variable plus equity. Because the grant counts towards its own denominator, the requirement is grossed up: 15 % of the OTE equivalent equals 17,6 % of base plus variable.
